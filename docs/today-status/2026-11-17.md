**Completed — Splash Screen**
- **The splash screen page matches the new design.** Hosts can set the title, subtitle, date, logo, background (picture, gradient or solid colour), colours, animation and music, with a live phone preview.
- **Templates and borders come from the admin portal.** Quick Templates are the admin's invitation templates, and the border styles are the admin's frames. A new splash starts with the same look as the event's invitation.
- **Hosts choose what shows and in what order.** Event title, couple names, logo, decoration, tagline, date and venue each have an on/off switch and can be dragged into order.
- **Display time is 5 to 30 seconds.**
- **Live tested.** The full splash page passed on the live site.
